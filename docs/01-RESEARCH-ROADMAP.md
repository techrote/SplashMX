## Active planning rule

Production work now follows `docs/architecture/IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP-V1.md`. It begins with frozen-contract guardrails and the smallest coherent local/offline canonical core, keeps simple browser authoring continuously testable, and only then expands into packages/publishing, measured Godot bindings, physical sandbox hardening, collaboration, production multiplayer, and distribution/service breadth.

Cloud services, registries/marketplaces, final CRDT/OT/database choice, final physical package/document encoding, production network deployment, and broad product polish are not implied prerequisites for the local/offline core merely because the research programme investigated their semantics.
