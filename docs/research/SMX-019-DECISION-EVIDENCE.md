## Decisions

- **D-111 — Progressive disclosure remains one semantic system in the browser slice.** Stage, Rules, Components, Together, People, Publish and Inspect project accepted Thing/Behaviour/Connection/Definition/Timeline records rather than owning separate beginner schemas.
- **D-112 — Ordinary browser Publish is immutable data projection.** A separately deployed generic player consumes the exact publication; no per-creation build/export belongs to the author path.
- **D-113 — Editable, preview, presence and published runtime planes remain separate.** Play state and presence do not become authored/published state; durable conflict alternatives remain editable-project history.
- **D-114 — Together projects the accepted topology-independent runtime declaration.** Simple and advanced controls change the same fields; transient transport identity remains context.
- **D-115 — Browser storage/offline mechanisms are non-semantic and fail typed.** Local key/value storage, service workers, CacheStorage and relay identity may be replaced without changing canonical identities.
- **D-116 — Protected source/audio/provenance remains one indivisible revision.** Save, Publish, cache, offline and peer paths preserve the complete AssetId revision bundle.

## Corrective finding

- **R-019-01 — Preserve canonical ConnectionId while excluding transport connection handles.** See the fixture file and deterministic test 28. The repair is a semantic-name boundary correction, not a weakening of host/transport isolation.

Two CI-discovered harness defects were also corrected without changing accepted semantics: progressive-disclosure controls are now reached through their visible `Together`/`People` disclosure surfaces before hidden controls are exercised, and peer convergence is checked against the declared replicated Thing state rather than authority-local executor event history. These are test-oracle corrections, not new canonical records or weakened network invariants.
